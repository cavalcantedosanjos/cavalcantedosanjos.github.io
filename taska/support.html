Getting Started

How do I open Taska?

After installing Taska, click its icon in your Mac menu bar (top-right area of the screen). The Kanban board will open instantly as a popover.

How do I add a task?

Click the + Add Task button at the top of the board, or press ⌘N to open a new task quickly. Fill in the title, and optionally set a due date and priority. Press ⌘Return to save.

How do I move a task between columns?

Drag and drop any task card from one column (To Do, In Progress, Done) to another. The change is saved automatically.

Notifications & Reminders

How do I get reminded about due dates?

Taska uses macOS Notifications to alert you when a task is due. You can set reminders to fire at the exact due time or a few minutes before. Open Taska's Settings to configure reminder timing.

I'm not seeing notifications. What should I check?

Make sure Taska has permission to send notifications. Go to System Settings → Notifications → Taska and ensure notifications are enabled.

Data & Privacy

Where is my data stored?

All your tasks are stored locally on your Mac using Apple's built-in storage system. Your data never leaves your device.

Does Taska sync with iCloud or other services?

No. Taska V1 is fully offline and does not sync anywhere. Your tasks are only available on the Mac where you installed the app.

How do I delete all my data?

Uninstalling Taska from your Mac removes all stored data. You can also clear individual tasks by moving them to the Done column and letting auto-clear remove them.

What data does Taska collect?

None. Taska collects no personal data, sends nothing to any server, and requires no account. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Settings

How do I make Taska launch automatically on login?

Open Taska's Settings (click the gear icon in the popover) and enable Launch at Login.

Can I turn off the auto-clear of the Done column?

Yes. In Settings, toggle off Auto-clear Done column. When disabled, completed tasks will stay in the Done column until you delete them manually.

How do I change the reminder timing?

In Settings, find Reminder Timing and choose your preferred option — at the exact due time, or 5, 15, or 30 minutes before.

Troubleshooting

Taska's icon doesn't appear in the menu bar.

Try quitting and relaunching the app. If your menu bar is crowded, macOS may be hiding some icons — look for the overflow indicator (») at the right end of the menu bar.

The app isn't responding or feels slow.

Quit Taska from the popover menu and relaunch it. If the problem persists, contact support with a description of what happened.
