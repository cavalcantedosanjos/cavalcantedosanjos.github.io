Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 29, 2026

Taska does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your Mac.

Overview

Taska ("the app") is a macOS menu bar application developed by Joao Anjos. This privacy policy explains how Taska handles your information. The short version: it doesn't collect any.

Data We Collect

Taska collects no personal data whatsoever. The app does not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or share any personally identifiable information.

Data Storage

All task data you create within Taska — including task titles, due dates, and priority levels — is stored exclusively on your local Mac using Apple's standard UserDefaults storage mechanism. This data never leaves your device.

Taska does not use iCloud, external databases, third-party analytics services, or any cloud storage of any kind.

Internet Access

Taska does not make any network requests. The app operates entirely offline and does not communicate with any external servers, APIs, or services.

Notifications

Taska may send local macOS notifications to remind you of upcoming task due dates. These notifications are generated entirely on your device using Apple's local notification system. No notification content is sent to any server or third party.

Third-Party Services

Taska does not integrate with any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or data brokers. There are no third parties involved in the operation of this app.

Children's Privacy

Taska does not collect any information from anyone, including children. The app is suitable for all ages.

Data Deletion

To delete all data stored by Taska, simply uninstall the app from your Mac. Since all data is stored locally on your device, uninstalling the app removes it completely.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes in the future, the updated policy will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date. Given the nature of the app (no data collection), substantive changes are unlikely.
