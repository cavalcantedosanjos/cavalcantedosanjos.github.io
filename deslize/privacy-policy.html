Deslize – Slide Puzzle

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

Deslize does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Your progress stays on your device.

Overview

Deslize ("the app") is an iOS slide puzzle game developed by Joao Anjos. This privacy policy explains how Deslize handles your information. The short version: it doesn't collect any. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data Stored on Your Device

Deslize stores only what it needs to play:

Game progress — your best star rating for each level you have solved.
Settings — whether haptic feedback is turned on.

This information is saved exclusively on your device using Apple's standard on-device storage. It is never sent to Joao Anjos or any third party (though it may be included in a standard iCloud or computer backup of your device that you control).

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, Deslize reports your scores to Game Center leaderboards and your progress to Game Center achievements. This data is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; Deslize never receives your Game Center account details or contacts. You can stop sharing at any time by signing out of Game Center in the iOS Settings app. The game works fully without it.

Internet Access

Deslize itself makes no network requests. All 200 puzzles are included in the app and it works completely offline. The only online feature is Game Center, which is provided by Apple as described above.

Third-Party Services

Deslize does not include any third-party SDKs, analytics frameworks, advertising networks, crash-reporting services, or data brokers. The only platform integration is Apple's Game Center.

Purchases

Deslize is free. It has no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or ads.

Children's Privacy

Deslize does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because the app gathers no data, it is suitable for players of all ages.

Data Deletion

To delete everything Deslize has stored, delete the app from your device. This removes your progress and settings. Scores and achievements already reported to Game Center are managed by Apple and can be reviewed from your Game Center profile.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the revised version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date. Any significant change that affects your data will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
