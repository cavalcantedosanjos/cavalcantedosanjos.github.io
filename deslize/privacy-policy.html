Deslize – Slide Puzzle

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

Deslize has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. It sends anonymous gameplay statistics to help tune the levels, and you can turn that off at any time in Settings › Privacy.

Overview

Deslize ("the app") is a slide puzzle game for iPhone and iPad developed by Joao Anjos. This policy explains what the app stores, what it sends, who receives it and how you can control it. Deslize never asks for your name, email address, phone number or contacts.

Data Stored on Your Device

To play, Deslize keeps the following on your device:

Game progress — your stars for each level in every mode, your best moves and times, and your daily challenge results and streak.
Game state — your hearts and how many hints you have left.
Settings — sound, haptics, appearance, controls, app icon and notification choices.
Identifiers — a random install ID and your player code (see below).

The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and the Live Activity read your hearts and progress from storage shared between the app and its widgets on the same device. This data may also be included in an iCloud or computer backup of your device that you control.

Gameplay Statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

Deslize uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the puzzles play, for example which levels take too long or are retried most. It records events such as starting, solving, retrying or leaving a level, the moves and time taken, the hints used, the mode and world you're playing, and when a world or mode unlocks. Like most apps that use Firebase, it also sends basic technical information: device model, iOS version, app version, language and an approximate country or region.

These statistics are tied to a random app instance ID and to your player code, a short code such as K7Q2-9XMD shown in Settings › Privacy. The player code is created on your device with a one-way hash of your Game Center player ID (or of the random install ID if you don't use Game Center). Your Game Center ID itself never leaves your device, and the code can't be turned back into it.

Deslize does not collect your name, email address, Apple Account, contacts, precise location, the advertising identifier (IDFA) or the identifier for vendor (IDFV). The statistics are not used for advertising, are not combined with data from other apps or websites, and are not sold or shared with data brokers. Your device's IP address reaches Google when the data is sent; Google Analytics doesn't store IP addresses. Statistics are kept for at most 14 months.

To stop sending gameplay statistics, turn off Share anonymous usage in Settings › Privacy.

Game Center and iCloud

If you're signed in to Game Center, Deslize reports your scores to leaderboards, unlocks achievements and saves a copy of your progress through Game Center, which keeps it in your iCloud Drive so it can be restored after you reinstall the app or move to a new device. This data is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. Deslize never receives your Game Center account details or contacts. The game works fully without Game Center; sign out under Settings › Game Center in the iOS Settings app to stop.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Deslize can remind you when your hearts have refilled or a new daily challenge is waiting. These reminders are scheduled on your device and no data is sent to deliver them. You can turn them off in the iOS Settings app at any time.

Service Providers

Gameplay statistics are processed by Google as part of Firebase. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and Google's Privacy Policy. Game Center and iCloud are provided by Apple. Deslize uses no other third-party services.

Purchases and Ads

Deslize is free. It has no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no ads.

Children's Privacy

Deslize doesn't ask anyone, including children, for their name, age, contact details or any other personal information. The gameplay statistics described above work the same way for every player and can be turned off in Settings › Privacy.

Your Choices and Data Deletion

Stop sharing statistics: turn off Share anonymous usage in Settings › Privacy.
Delete data on your device: delete the app. This removes your progress, settings and identifiers.
Delete your saved game: remove Deslize's data from your iCloud storage in the iOS Settings app.
Delete collected statistics: email the address below with your player code from Settings › Privacy, and the statistics tied to it will be deleted.

Scores and achievements already reported to Game Center are managed by Apple and can be reviewed from your Game Center profile.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the revised version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date. Any significant change that affects your data will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.

Português

O Deslize não tem contas, anúncios nem rastreamento, e nunca pede seu nome, e-mail ou contatos. Para ajustar as fases, o app envia estatísticas anônimas de jogo ao Google Analytics for Firebase (níveis iniciados, resolvidos e repetidos, movimentos, tempo, dicas, além do modelo do aparelho, versão do iOS, idioma e país aproximado), associadas a um ID aleatório e ao seu código do jogador, criado no aparelho a partir de um hash irreversível. Para parar, desative Compartilhar uso anônimo em Ajustes › Privacidade. Com o Game Center, pontuações, conquistas e uma cópia do progresso (no seu iCloud Drive) são tratadas pela Apple. As notificações são agendadas no próprio aparelho. Para apagar os dados do aparelho, exclua o app; para apagar as estatísticas coletadas, envie seu código do jogador para o e-mail acima.
